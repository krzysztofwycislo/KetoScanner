KetoScanner test barcodes

Real EAN-13 barcodes, each checked against Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) on 2026-10-27. Scan them from the screen at full brightness, or print the page. Carb values are total carbohydrates per 100 g as Open Food Facts listed them that day; the verdict the app shows depends on your diet preferences.

Low carb (0–5 g per 100 g)

Medium (5–15 g per 100 g)

High carb (over 15 g per 100 g)
