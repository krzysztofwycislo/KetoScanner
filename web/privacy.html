Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what data the CarbPeek app for Android ("the app") and this website use, why, and what your rights are. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and most of your data never leaves your phone.

Who is responsible

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Krzysztof Wycisło, a private person. You can reach us at support@carbpeek.app.

Data that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • your diet profile and daily carb limit, and whether you finished the first setup;
  • your scan history and favourites;
  • a cache of product information, so products you scanned before open quickly.

We cannot see this data. It is not included in Android backups. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

Camera

The app asks for camera access to read barcodes. Barcodes are recognised on your phone; the camera picture is not stored or sent anywhere. The camera is only active while the scan screen is open. The app also uses the camera, or the Android photo picker, when you choose to add photos to a report (see below).

Looking up products

When you scan a product, the app sends its barcode to Open Food Facts, an open food database run by a non-profit association in France, and loads the product information and product images from its servers. The request contains only the barcode and the app name and version. No account, name or device identifier is sent. As with any internet request, the server receives your IP address. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

In the future the app will look up products through our own servers in the EU instead. We will update this policy before that happens.

Reports and photos

You can report a problem with a product, or a missing product, and add photos of the label and a note. Reports are always sent by you, never automatically. They contain the barcode, the photos and the text you enter; we ask you not to include personal information in photos or notes.

Reports are sent to our own servers in the EU, where a person reviews them to correct or add the product. We do not pass your photos or notes to Open Food Facts or anyone else. Reports are deleted once the product is added or the report is closed, and at the latest after 12 months.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping product information correct (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and your request when you send a report.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or crash reporting in the app. Errors are only logged on your phone.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Service providers

To run our servers and this website we use:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. for DNS, network protection, file storage and hosting of this website;
  • a hosting provider with data centres in the EU for our servers.

They process data only on our behalf and under data processing agreements. Where data may be processed outside the EU, this is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

This website

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties. Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected and have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live.

To use these rights, email support@carbpeek.app. As most data stays on your phone, we usually only hold reports you have sent; tell us the barcode and roughly when you sent the report so we can find it.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it handles data, and change the date at the top.
