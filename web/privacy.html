Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what data the CarbPeek app for Android ("the app") and this website use, why, and what your rights are. In short: there is no account and no advertising, and most of your data never leaves your phone.

Who is responsible

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Krzysztof Wycisło, a private person. You can reach us at support@carbpeek.app.

Data that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • your diet profile and daily carb limit, and whether you finished the first setup;
  • your scan history and favourites;
  • a cache of product information and images, so products you scanned before open quickly;
  • photos you add to a report, until you send or close it.

We cannot see this data. It is not included in Android cloud backups. Some phones can still copy it when you move directly to a new phone. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

Camera

The app asks for camera access to read barcodes. Barcodes are recognised on your phone; the scanning picture is not stored or sent anywhere. The app also uses the camera, or the Android photo picker, when you choose to add photos to a report. The camera is only active while one of these screens is open.

Barcode recognition by Google ML Kit

The app reads barcodes with ML Kit, a software library from Google. It works on your phone, and your camera picture and the barcode are not sent to Google. ML Kit does send Google technical data about its use: device model and Android version, the app's name and version, performance and error information, and an identifier of this installation that, according to Google, is not intended to identify you or your phone. We do not receive this data. See what ML Kit collects and the Google privacy policy.

Looking up products

When you scan a product, or open a saved product whose information is older than 30 days, the app loads its information from Open Food Facts, an open food database run by a non-profit association in France. Product images come from there too. The requests contain the barcode or the address of the image, the details we ask for, a language setting that is the same for everyone, and the app's name, version and our contact address. No account, name or device identifier is sent. As with any internet request, the server receives your IP address. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Reports and photos

You can report a problem with a product, or a missing product, and add photos of the label and a note. We ask you not to include personal information in photos or notes.

In the current version reports are not sent to us yet: what you enter stays on your phone, and the photos are deleted when you send or close the report. Before reports are sent to us, we will update this policy. We will then keep them until the product is added or the report is closed, and at most 12 months.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of our own in the app. Errors are only logged on your phone, apart from the ML Kit data described above.
  • We do not sell your data.

Service providers

Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) delivers this website and forwards email sent to support@carbpeek.app. Cloudflare states in its customer data processing addendum that it complies with the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and uses the EU standard contractual clauses.

If you email us

Email to support@carbpeek.app is forwarded by Cloudflare to our mailbox provider. We use your address and message only to answer you.

This website

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties. Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks. If a page cannot be loaded, your browser may report the network error to Cloudflare.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected and have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live.

To use these rights, email support@carbpeek.app. As most data stays on your phone, we usually only hold emails you have sent us.

Changes

We will update this page before the app changes how it handles data, and change the date at the top.
