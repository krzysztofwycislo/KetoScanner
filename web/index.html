CarbPeek

Scan a product, see if it fits your diet.

CarbPeek is a shopping assistant for people on a keto or low-carb diet. Point the camera at a barcode and get a clear verdict, without reading the nutrition label in the shop aisle.

Every product gets a clear verdict and the main reason behind it. Choose Strict keto, Keto or Low carb, or set your own daily carb limit.

Coming soon to Android

Product information comes from Open Food Facts, a free, collaborative database, available under the Open Database License.

CarbPeek is a shopping aid, not medical or dietary advice.
