Beyond the carb count

A verdict you can trust

CarbPeek looks at the carbs, fat and protein of each product and compares them with your diet. When the ingredient list is known, it also checks for added sugar, starch or flour and polyol sweeteners, so hidden carbs don't slip into your basket.
