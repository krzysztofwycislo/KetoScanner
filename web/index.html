Scan a product, see if it fits your diet.

CarbPeek is a shopping assistant for keto and low-carb diets. Point your phone at a barcode and get a clear verdict in the shop aisle, without studying the nutrition label.

Coming soon to Android

How it works

  1. Pick your diet once: Strict keto, Keto or Low-carb, or set your own daily carb limit.
  2. Scan the barcode of any packaged food.
  3. Get a verdict and the main reason behind it.
  • Keto-friendly
  • In small amounts
  • Not keto-friendly

The verdict looks at the carbs, fat and protein of the product and compares them with your diet. When the ingredient list is known, CarbPeek also checks it for added sugar, starch or flour and polyol sweeteners, so hidden carbs don't slip into your basket.
